About

I grew up in Mumbai, India, and eventually moved to York County, Pennsylvania, where I have lived ever since. It was in Mumbai where I saw firsthand how numbers change lives through the city's extensive network of bazaars and commercial districts. That interest grew into a desire to understand how financial knowledge can drive growth in a business. While at Wharton, I hope to build on this drive by developing skills to serve others and create economic value.

Outside coursework, I run Forever Flowers, a small nonprofit I started in high school that sells hand-folded origami flowers to raise money for the WellSpan York Cancer Center. At Penn, I serve as Community Outreach Chair for VITA@Penn, the student chapter of the IRS's Volunteer Income Tax Assistance program, where I help connect volunteers with partner sites across Philadelphia and keep the club's outreach and recruitment running. In my free time, I am an active board member for the Social Deduction Club (SDC), a recreational club focused on and hidden-role and mafia-esque games.
